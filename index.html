Making charge and tax (all reels)

The GST rate and how it applies to making charges must match your actual billing: ask your CA. Reels show an estimate; the final price is as per your bill. If you charge wastage or any other fee, show it, so the flat making-charge claim stays true.

Shop details

Cash only / No udhar badge (shown on every reel)

Important: Section 269ST bars a jeweller from receiving ₹2 lakh or more in cash from one person in a day, for one transaction, or for one occasion, and the penalty equals the amount received. PAN is also required for jewellery purchases of ₹2 lakh and above, in any payment mode. So "cash only" cannot apply to big bills: accept bank transfer, UPI or cheque for them, and say so in your terms. Ask your CA to confirm for your shop.

Silver coin offer (shown on every reel)

Keep the offer honest: say exactly which purchases qualify (for example a minimum bill) in your disclaimer, and describe the coin accurately (weight, purity) only if you can show it. Ask your CA how a free gift is treated for GST and billing.
